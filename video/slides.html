How the AI is used

Gemini plans. The code checks the maths.

  • Plan: Gemini 3.6 Flash writes the summary, budget shifts, checklists, vendors and theme as structured JSON.
  • Budget: Gemini suggests the split; our budget engine applies it, so the total always adds up and scales with guest count.
  • Chat: plain-English requests become exact budget moves, new tasks, vendors and activities.
  • Safety net: AI output is validated before display, with a template plan if Gemini is unavailable.
